The <info>%command.name%</info> command generates CI/CD configuration files for your
Symfony application.

  <info>php %command.full_name%</info>

The command inspects your <comment>composer.json</comment> and <comment>.env</comment> to detect installed tools
(PHPUnit, PHP-CS-Fixer, PHPStan, Twig CS Fixer, Doctrine, etc.) and generates an
appropriate CI workflow.

You can specify the CI platform using the <comment>--platform</comment> option:

  <info>php %command.full_name% --platform=github-actions</info>
  <info>php %command.full_name% --platform=gitlab-ci</info>

For <comment>GitHub Actions</comment>:
  - <info>.github/workflows/ci.yaml</info>: workflow with lint, test, and analysis jobs

For <comment>GitLab CI/CD</comment>:
  - <info>.gitlab-ci.yml</info>: pipeline with lint and test stages
